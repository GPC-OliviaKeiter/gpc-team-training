# LinkedIn Playbook

## Why organic content

Trust and credibility: Grant talks daily about the pain points GPC's clients
face, so readers recognize themselves in it. Sustainable growth: high-quality
content plus 100+ weekly network adds compounds followers over months, on top
of newsletter features, tool partnerships, and a podcast. Community
building: GPC gets recognized as an industry expert, invited to communities,
newsletters, and events. Organic is free compared to paid ads, and holds up
as long as the content mix stays trending, educational, personal, and social
proof.

## Posting rhythm

Post at 2:45 PM EST. Comment on your own post for 30 minutes before and
after it goes up. The first comment under every post is a fixed line linking
to past work. Respond to every comment immediately, not on a delay.

## The commenting SOP

**Objective:** establish GPC as a thought leader in automation and AI,
build relationships, and spark conversation, always leading with value, not
a pitch.

**Voice:** professional but conversational, insightful and thought-
provoking, engaging and value-driven (contribute, don't just react),
authentic and human, concise.

**Which posts to engage:** from potential clients, partners, and industry
leaders; content aligned with automation, AI, process efficiency, or startup
growth; posts mentioning tools GPC uses regularly (like Fathom). Avoid
competitors' posts unless it's a neutral industry discussion. It's fine to
mention a GPC solution when it actually adds value to the thread, not as a
generic plug.

**Structure:** acknowledge the post's idea, add value with a real
perspective or experience, then encourage engagement with a follow-up
question.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| 1-3 sentences with a real point | Generic praise alone ("Great post!") |
| A question at the end to invite the sale, not a pitch upfront | Leading with "Check out GPC, we can solve this" |
| Links at the end of the comment | A link in the middle, breaking the flow |
| Break long thoughts into short lines | One dense paragraph |
| Reference a competitor's good idea in your own post | Comment directly on a competitor's post |

After posting, track engagement (likes, replies, profile views); if a
comment underperforms, analyze timing, content, or structure before the next
one.

**Keywords that find good posts:** Fathom, AI meeting assistant, meeting
transcripts, sales calls, ClickUp, repurposing content, content creation,
HubSpot, switching tools, agency owner. Less frequently useful: project
management, consulting, project manager, automation, AI, marketing agency.
