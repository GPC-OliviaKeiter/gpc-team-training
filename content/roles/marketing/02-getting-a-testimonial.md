# Getting a Testimonial

1. Reach out within 1 day of an inflection point, the moment a client just
   got a great result or experience.
2. Use a scheduling link to book a 15-minute call within the next 2
   business days.
3. On the call, walk the client through the Epiphany Bridge framework
   below.
4. Ask permission to use the recording in marketing. That's the whole
   point of running the call.

<Callout>A quote with no "before" state ("They made us $1 million!") lands flat. Pairing it with the struggle beforehand is what makes the after-state land. Always draw out the contrast.</Callout>

## The Epiphany Bridge framework

| Question group | Ask |
|---|---|
| 1. Who they are | Name, and where they live or their business |
| 2. External, before | How was your situation before? (1-2 short statements) |
| 3. Internal, before | How did that make you feel? What couldn't you do that you wished you could? What negative impact did it have on others around you? |
| 4. The bridge | How did you find us? What were your doubts or fears? What made you say yes anyway? |
| 5. External, after | Where are you now? What can you do now that you couldn't before? What positive impact has that had on others? |
| 6. Call to action | What would you tell someone watching this who's unsure about taking the next step? |

Build the final video around the before-state contrast and close on the
call-to-action answer.
