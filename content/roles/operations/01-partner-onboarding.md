# Partner Onboarding: OM Responsibilities

The Operations Manager owns three financial-tracking subtasks during a
partner's onboarding, interleaved with the Project Manager's and Process
Consultant's own. See
[Partner Lifecycle: Onboarding to Offboarding](/roles/project-management/partner-lifecycle)
in the Project Management track for the full end-to-end sequence and the
swimlane diagram; this page covers only the Operations Manager's slice of
Phase 2.

| # | Subtask | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Confirm Payment Initiation | Verify the initial payment has actually been initiated for the engagement |
| 2 | Update Airtable Base | Add or update the client's information in the Airtable base, the same fields for every new client |
| 3 | Receive First Invoice Payment | Confirm receipt of the first invoice payment for the SOW |

<Callout>Leave "Receive First Invoice Payment" open until payment is actually confirmed. The invoice being sent isn't enough to mark it complete.</Callout>

<Callout>Once every onboarding subtask across the Project Manager, Operations Manager, and Process Consultant is marked complete, ClickUp automatically moves the partner to Active status.</Callout>
