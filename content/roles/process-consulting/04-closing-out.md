# Closing Out

When a partner's Account Status changes to **Close Out** in ClickUp, three
action items generate automatically, assigned to the Process Consultant.
Completing them triggers the automatic move into Offboarding, owned from
there by the Project Manager or Operations Manager.

| # | Action | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Send the post-deployment offer | Include any upsell or cross-sell opportunities (see Identifying & Pitching Upsell Opportunities). Ideally these surfaced earlier in the engagement, not for the first time here. |
| 2 | Conduct the closeout alignment meeting | Confirm every deliverable in the signed SOW is complete and explicitly accepted by the client, not just marked done internally. |
| 3 | Send the post-engagement feedback form | Pulled from the NPS list in the partner's ClickUp folder. Separate and later than the mid-engagement NPS check in Managing the Relationship. |

<Figure id="pc-closeout-tasks-generated" caption="The three auto-generated Close Out subtasks on a partner record, assigned to the Process Consultant" spec="ClickUp, the partner record, Close Out status, the three PC subtasks visible" />

<Callout>Flag completion to your Project Manager or Operations Manager so they can mark the corresponding subtasks complete. You don't mark your own.</Callout>

When every Close Out subtask across the team is complete, the automation
moves the partner from Close Out to Offboarding and generates the
Offboarding checklist.

Your responsibility for the account ends there, unless the closeout meeting
or the feedback form surfaces something that needs further client
follow-up.
