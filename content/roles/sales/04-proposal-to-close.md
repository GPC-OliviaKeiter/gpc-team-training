# Proposal to Close

## Pricing and discount authority

Sales can quote GPC's current standard pricing without approval, always from the
[Product Guide](https://app.clickup.com/9012022270/v/dc/8cjh2zy-176272/8cjh2zy-101552)
(the GPC Wiki's source of truth), never from an old proposal, a previous client
agreement, Slack, or memory.

Anything outside standard pricing needs approval from the **Operations Manager
or CEO**, before it reaches the prospect in a conversation, a proposal, an SOW,
or a signature request: lowering the standard price, a special rate, a waived
fee, free scope or hours, non-standard payment terms, partner or nonprofit
pricing, or bundled pricing below the standard combined price. A request for
approval includes lead name, product, standard price, proposed price, and the
reason.

<Callout>Sales can quote standard pricing. Anything outside it needs Operations Manager or CEO approval before it reaches the prospect, not after.</Callout>

## Proposal, SOW, and signature

**Verbal Yes → Pricing Approval → PandaDoc SOW → Out for Signature → Fully
Signed → Attach Contract → Closed Won → Automated Onboarding → Delivery
Handoff.**

| Step | Owner | What has to be true |
|---|---|---|
| Confirm the verbal yes | Closer | Product, tier, pricing, scope, and timing agreed in principle, before the SOW gets created |
| Confirm pricing and approvals | Closer | Pricing matches the approved product structure; any discount already approved by Ops Manager or CEO |
| Create the PandaDoc SOW | Closer | Full commercial agreement: product, scope, deliverables, pricing, term, payment terms, signature fields. The MNDA is separate, not inside the SOW |
| Route for signature | Closer, CEO signs for GPC | Deal moves to Out for Signature. That is not Closed Won |
| Follow up until signed | Closer | Monitor status, answer questions, route material changes for approval instead of a side conversation |
| Confirm the executed agreement | Closer | Both sides signed; signed contract attached to the HubSpot deal, required before it can advance |
| Move to Closed Won | Closer, manually | Triggers the automated client onboarding email |
| Trigger the Sales-to-Delivery handoff | Closer | See below |

## Closed Won → Delivery Handoff

The handoff has to cover everything Delivery would otherwise have to
reconstruct: signed SOW, product and tier, contract value and term, final
scope, and any approved exception; the key client stakeholders (Executive
Sponsor, Primary Contact, other decision-makers, a known skeptic if there is
one); discovery context (why they engaged, the pain, the desired outcome, the
workflows and systems discussed); and every commitment made during sales
(timing, deliverables, special requests, deadlines).

<Callout>If Delivery would be surprised to hear it from the client, it belongs in the handoff.</Callout>

| Product sold | Delivery team |
|---|---|
| Workshop | Workshop Process Consultant + Project Manager |
| Workflow Assessment | Workflow Process Consultant + Project Manager |
| AI Adoption | Workshop or Workflow Process Consultant + Project Manager |
| Technical Build | Workflow Process Consultant + Project Manager + Engineering |
| Growth Insurance / legacy | Assigned account and delivery owners |

<Figure id="sales-hubspot-closed-won" caption="A deal at Closed Won in HubSpot with the signed contract attached" spec="HubSpot, a Closed Won deal, attachments panel showing the signed SOW" />

The handoff completes within 2 business days of signature. Delivery reviews it
before kickoff; anything unclear gets resolved internally, never silently
absorbed as new scope, an undocumented promise, or an unapproved timeline.
Once complete: Sales owns the commercial history, Delivery owns execution.

## The Sales → Delivery Handoff template

A fill-in form the Closer completes per deal, in this order: Deal Information,
Why They Bought, Scope, Key Client Stakeholders, Commitments Made During Sales,
Discovery Context, Known Risks and Sensitivities, Immediate Next Steps, Final
Handoff Check. See the
[live template](https://app.clickup.com/9012022270/v/dc/8cjh2zy-180892/8cjh2zy-112612)
in ClickUp for the exact fields.

<Callout>Final handoff question, every time: "Is there anything the client expects that isn't clearly documented in the signed SOW or this handoff?" A yes gets resolved before the handoff counts as done.</Callout>
